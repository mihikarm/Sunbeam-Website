Who is Sunbeam-Charlotte for?

Teen girls (girl-identifying), 13-18, that are still in high school!

What IS Sunbeam-Charlotte?

Sunbeam is a free all-teen-girls social coding event run by a 501(c)(3) nonprofit called Hack Club, which is backed up by Dell, AMD, NASA and GitHub!! There will be 40+ Sunbeams occuring around the world. We aim to build on whatever coding skills you have, stellar or nonexistent!!

Where is Sunbeam being held?

Sunbeam Charlotte will be held at the South County Regional Library, 5801 Rea Rd, Charlotte, NC 28277. The event will be held in Community Room B (located upstairs)

Why is Sunbeam being held?

To empower the next generation of Women in STEM!!

When is Sunbeam?

Sunbeam Charlotte will be held on August 29th, 2026 from 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM
